What Stupen is

A practice coach. It plans what to practise, builds exercises and coaches you through them using an AI model on your own account. The coach is a language model and can be wrong, about the subject and about how much it helps. Check what it tells you.

Your AI account

You connect your own provider account or API key. The provider bills you for what you use, under its own terms. Keep an eye on your usage and spending limits there.

Fair use

Don't use Stupen to:

  • attack, scan or overload other systems, including from lesson code or containers;
  • mine cryptocurrency or run anything unrelated to learning in the code runner;
  • get around the limits, or into other people's accounts or data;
  • store or share anything illegal or anything you have no right to share.

Accounts have limits on checks, generated lessons, coach messages and storage. We may disable an account that breaks these terms or puts the service at risk.
